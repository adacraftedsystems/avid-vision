The AI Advantage

What AI actually does
for an advisor like you.

Not buzzwords. Specific capabilities that cut hours from your week and make your clients feel like they have a team of 10 working for them.

📊
Automated Client Prep Packets Save 3 hrs/client
Before every review meeting, AI pulls together portfolio performance, goal progress, market context, and 3 agenda items tailored to that client's situation. You review and show up ready.
💬
AI Client Inbox Assistant After hours coverage
Client emails at 11pm asking about market volatility get an intelligent, empathetic first response. You see the thread in the morning and add your personal touch. Clients feel heard 24/7.
📈
Portfolio Anomaly Detection Risk management
AI monitors all client portfolios daily. Flags drift, tax loss harvesting opportunities, rebalancing needs, and life event triggers. You focus on the outliers — AI watches everything else.
📝
Financial Plan Drafting 10x faster
Input client data → AI generates a complete first-draft financial plan with projections, scenarios, and recommendations. You edit and personalize. What took 6 hours takes 1.
🎯
Proactive Client Outreach Referral engine
AI identifies which clients haven't heard from you recently, cross-references life events (job changes, marriages, new babies via LinkedIn), and drafts personalized check-in messages for your review.
🔮
Scenario Modeling in Seconds Client WOW factor
"What if I buy a rental property?" — AI runs 5 scenarios live in your meeting. "What if I retire 3 years early?" — same. Clients leave meetings feeling like they have a supercomputer working for them.
